## ENGAGEMENT HACKS

1. **Always end captions with a question** — "Which one are you?" / "Tag that friend" / "Comment your best rec"
2. **Post at 7-9 PM** (when people are deciding what to watch)
3. **Reply to EVERY comment** in the first hour — algorithm loves this
4. **Share memes to your Story** after posting with "New post!" sticker
5. **Use 3-5 hashtags max** (Instagram 2025/2026 = fewer hashtags, more keywords in caption)
6. **Collab post** with friends' accounts for 2x audience reach
7. **Save audio** from trending Reels for your own Reels later
