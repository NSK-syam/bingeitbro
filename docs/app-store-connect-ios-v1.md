# BiB iOS v1 App Store Submission

This document captures the locked scope and the remaining manual App Store Connect inputs for the first iOS review submission.

## Locked iOS Scope

- Discovery only: auth, movies, shows, watchlist, reminders, legal, support, account deletion
- No ads in the iOS shell
- No payments in the iOS shell
- No songs hub in the iOS shell
- No trivia in the iOS shell
- No add-recommendation flow in the iOS shell
- No friend sends, nudges, group watch, or other social/chat surfaces in the iOS shell
